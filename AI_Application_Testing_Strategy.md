# AI Application Testing Strategy – QA Guides

A comprehensive guide for Quality Assurance professionals on testing various AI applications.

---

## Recommendation Engine – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Recommendation Relevance:** Create user profiles with distinct tastes and histories. Check if the recommendations align with those profiles by browsing and interacting with the app.
- ✅ **Test "Cold Start" Scenarios:** Simulate a new user with no history. Ensure the system provides sensible default or popular recommendations instead of crashing or showing empty results.
- ✅ **Assess Real-Time Updates:** Change user preferences or interact with new items. Confirm that the recommendation engine adapts and updates its suggestions in near real-time.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Validate Recommendation Explanations:** If the system explains its suggestions (e.g., "Because you liked X"), verify that the reasoning is logical and accurate by checking the user's interaction history.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Check for Popularity Bias:** Analyze recommendations over a period to ensure they don't exclusively feature popular items. Look for a healthy mix of popular and niche suggestions.
- ✅ **Audit for Unfair Bias:** Create diverse user personas (e.g., different demographics). Check for any systemic biases in the recommendations that might favor one group over another.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Confirm Data Privacy:** Review the data handling process to ensure that personally identifiable information (PII) is anonymized and protected throughout the recommendation pipeline.

## Semantic Search – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test for Conceptual Understanding:** Use synonyms, paraphrased questions, and conceptually related terms instead of exact keywords. Verify that the search results are relevant to the query's intent.
- ✅ **Verify Ranking Relevance:** For a given query, ensure the top-ranked results are more relevant than lower-ranked ones by manually evaluating their content.
- ✅ **Check Handling of Ambiguous Queries:** Input queries with multiple meanings (e.g., "apple"). Confirm the system either provides results for the most common interpretation or offers disambiguation options.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Search Snippets:** Examine the snippets or highlighted text in the search results. Verify they accurately reflect why the result is considered relevant to the query.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Complex and Niche Queries:** Use long, detailed, or domain-specific queries. Ensure the search engine can handle complexity without a significant drop in relevance.
- ✅ **Assess for Bias in Results:** Search for neutral terms and analyze the results for any skew towards a particular viewpoint, product, or demographic.

### Multi-language Support
- ✅ **Validate Multilingual Queries:** If applicable, translate a set of test queries into supported languages. Run the searches and verify that the results are contextually and culturally appropriate.

## Data-to-Insights Copilot – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Data Interpretation:** Provide a structured dataset and ask the copilot to generate insights. Manually verify the accuracy of its interpretations, calculations, and summaries.
- ✅ **Test Chart and Graph Generation:** Request visualizations (e.g., "Show me a bar chart of sales by region"). Check that the correct data is used and the chart type is appropriate.
- ✅ **Validate Natural Language Queries:** Ask complex questions about the data in plain English. Confirm the copilot correctly translates the question into a data query and returns an accurate answer.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Check for Source-Data Linking:** Ensure that every insight or data point generated by the copilot links back to the source data. Verify this by clicking through or cross-referencing.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Incomplete or Messy Data:** Input datasets with missing values, outliers, or incorrect formatting. Observe how the copilot handles these imperfections and whether it flags them.
- ✅ **Audit for Interpretation Bias:** Provide ambiguous data and check if the copilot's interpretation shows any bias (e.g., overly optimistic or pessimistic).

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Ensure Data Privacy in Outputs:** Verify that any generated insights or reports do not expose sensitive PII. Check for proper data masking and anonymization.

## Anomaly / Fraud Detection – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test with Known Fraudulent Cases:** Use a dataset of historical, confirmed fraud cases. Run them through the system and verify that it correctly flags them (True Positives).
- ✅ **Test with Legitimate Cases:** Use a dataset of known, legitimate transactions. Verify that the system does not incorrectly flag them as fraudulent (False Positives).
- ✅ **Assess Model Adaptability:** Introduce new or evolving patterns of fraud. Check if the model can adapt and learn to detect these new patterns over time.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Validate Anomaly Explanations:** For every flagged anomaly, review the explanation provided by the system. Ensure the reasoning is sound and points to the correct suspicious factors.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Check for Bias Against User Segments:** Analyze false positive rates across different user demographics or segments. Ensure the model isn't unfairly targeting a specific group.
- ✅ **Test System Performance Under High Load:** Simulate a high volume of transactions. Verify that the system's accuracy and response time remain within acceptable limits.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Confirm Secure Handling of Financial Data:** Audit the entire data pipeline to ensure sensitive financial and personal information is encrypted, access-controlled, and handled securely.

## Voice Assistant / Phone IVR – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test Speech Recognition in Various Environments:** Speak commands in quiet, noisy, and echo-prone environments. Test with different accents, dialects, and speaking speeds to check for recognition accuracy.
- ✅ **Verify Intent Recognition:** Speak commands that are phrased differently but have the same intent (e.g., "What's the weather?" vs. "Tell me the forecast"). Confirm the assistant responds correctly.
- ✅ **Assess Conversation Flow:** Engage in a multi-turn conversation. Verify the assistant remembers context, handles interruptions gracefully, and follows the designed conversation path.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Check for Clarification Questions:** When a command is ambiguous, verify the assistant asks clarifying questions instead of making an incorrect assumption.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Non-Standard Speech:** Use slang, filler words ("um," "ah"), or incomplete sentences. Check how the assistant handles these "real-world" speech patterns.
- ✅ **Audit for Voice-Based Bias:** Test with a diverse range of voices (e.g., different genders, ages, and pitches) to ensure equal recognition accuracy.

### Multi-language Support
- ✅ **Validate Language Switching:** If supported, switch between languages during a conversation. Verify the assistant recognizes the switch and responds in the correct language.

## Knowledge-Base Q&A – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Factual Accuracy:** Ask questions with known answers from the knowledge base. Confirm the AI provides correct, up-to-date information.
- ✅ **Test Handling of "No Answer" Scenarios:** Ask questions that are not covered in the knowledge base. Verify the AI responds gracefully (e.g., "I don't know the answer to that") instead of hallucinating an incorrect answer.
- ✅ **Check for Source Linking:** For every answer provided, ensure it includes a link or reference to the source document in the knowledge base. Click the link to verify its relevance.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Answer Snippets:** When the AI quotes a source, check if the highlighted snippet accurately represents the context of the answer.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Ambiguous or Poorly Phrased Questions:** Ask questions that are vague or contain typos. Assess the AI's ability to interpret the user's intent and provide a relevant answer or ask for clarification.
- ✅ **Audit for Biased Answers:** Ask questions on sensitive topics and review the answers for any political, social, or ideological bias.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Ensure No PII in Public Answers:** If the knowledge base contains sensitive information, verify that the Q&A system does not inadvertently expose PII in its answers to unauthorized users.

## Predictive Scoring / Forecasts – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Validate Against Historical Data:** Use a holdout dataset (historical data the model has not seen). Run the model's predictions and compare them against the actual outcomes to measure accuracy.
- ✅ **Test Model Stability:** Run the model multiple times with the same input data. Verify that the output scores or forecasts are consistent and reproducible.
- ✅ **Assess Forecast Time Horizon:** Generate forecasts for different time periods (e.g., next week, next quarter). Manually assess the plausibility of the forecasts based on domain knowledge.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Feature Importance:** For a given prediction, check the "key drivers" or "feature importance" explanations. Verify that the factors the model considers most important are logical and make sense to a domain expert.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Check for Bias in Scoring:** Analyze the distribution of scores across different demographic or customer segments. Ensure the model does not systematically give lower or higher scores to a particular group without a valid reason.
- ✅ **Test with Extreme or Outlier Data:** Input data points that are unusual or fall outside the normal range. Observe how the model handles them and whether the predictions are still stable.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Confirm Privacy of Input Data:** Ensure that the data used for generating scores is handled securely and that any PII is masked or anonymized where appropriate.

## Conversational AI Assistant – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test Context Retention:** Engage in a multi-turn conversation. Check if the assistant remembers previous user inputs and uses that context in subsequent responses.
- ✅ **Verify Tool/API Integration:** If the assistant uses external tools (e.g., booking an appointment, checking order status), test these functions thoroughly to ensure they work correctly.
- ✅ **Assess Graceful Failure:** When the assistant cannot fulfill a request, verify that it fails gracefully, provides a helpful message, and offers to escalate to a human agent if necessary.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Check for Response Justification:** In complex scenarios, ask the assistant "Why did you say that?". A good assistant should be able to provide some level of justification for its responses.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test for Toxic or Unsafe Responses:** Use adversarial prompts designed to elicit harmful, biased, or inappropriate content. Verify that the assistant's safety guardrails prevent it from generating such responses.
- ✅ **Check for Repetitive Loops:** Engage in long or confusing conversations. Ensure the assistant does not get stuck in a repetitive loop of canned responses.

### Multi-language Support
- ✅ **Verify Code-Switching:** Test conversations where the user mixes two or more languages in the same sentence. Check if the assistant can understand and respond appropriately.

## Generative Writing & Summaries – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Evaluate Factual Accuracy (Grounding):** For summaries or text generated from a source document, meticulously check that all claims, figures, and facts are accurate and correctly attributed to the source.
- ✅  **Assess Coherence and Readability:** Read the generated text aloud. Verify that it is fluent, well-structured, and easy to understand. Check for grammatical errors and awkward phrasing.
- ✅ **Test for Completeness in Summaries:** Generate a summary of a long document. Compare it to the original to ensure that all key points and critical information have been included.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Verify Source Linking in Summaries:** Ensure that every key point in a summary is linked back to the specific section of the source document it came from.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Audit for Plagiarism:** Use a plagiarism checker tool to compare the generated text against the source documents and public web content. Ensure the output is original and not just copied.
- ✅ **Check for Tone and Style Consistency:** Specify a particular tone or style in the prompt (e.g., "Write in a formal tone"). Verify that the generated text adheres to the requested style.
- ✅ **Test for Hallucinations:** In ungrounded generation, ask the model about niche or nonexistent topics. Verify that it admits to not knowing, rather than inventing false information.

## Code Assistant / Dev Copilot – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Correctness of Generated Code:** Generate code snippets for various tasks. Manually review and run the code to ensure it is syntactically correct and functionally does what was requested.
- ✅ **Test for Different Programming Languages:** If the assistant supports multiple languages, test its code generation capabilities across all of them.
- ✅ **Check for Contextual Awareness:** Open a project with existing code. Ask the copilot to add a new function that uses other functions in the codebase. Verify it understands the existing code and generates compatible suggestions.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Validate Code Explanations:** Ask the copilot to explain a piece of generated code. Verify the explanation is clear, accurate, and helpful for understanding the code's logic.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Audit for Security Vulnerabilities:** Use a static analysis security testing (SAST) tool to scan the generated code for common vulnerabilities (e.g., SQL injection, XSS).
- ✅ **Test for Inefficient or Non-Idiomatic Code:** Have a senior developer review the generated code. Check if it follows best practices, is reasonably efficient, and uses idiomatic patterns for the language.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Ensure Privacy of Code Context:** Verify that the code from the user's local environment, which is sent to the model as context, is handled securely and not stored or used for any other purpose.

## Document OCR – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test with Various Document Types:** Scan different types of documents (e.g., invoices, receipts, contracts, handwritten notes). Verify the accuracy of the extracted text for each type.
- ✅ **Check Accuracy on Low-Quality Images:** Use images that are blurry, skewed, have low contrast, or contain complex backgrounds. Measure the Character Error Rate (CER) to assess performance.
- ✅ **Verify Field-Specific Extraction:** For structured documents like invoices, verify that the OCR correctly identifies and extracts specific fields (e.g., Invoice Number, Total Amount, Date).

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Confidence Scores:** If the OCR provides confidence scores for extracted words or characters, manually check the low-confidence items to see if they correlate with actual errors.
- ✅ **Check Bounding Box Accuracy:** Visually inspect the bounding boxes drawn around the extracted text. Ensure they are tight and correctly segment words and lines.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Different Languages and Scripts:** If the OCR supports multiple languages, test it with documents in each language, including those with non-Latin scripts.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Confirm Secure Handling of Scanned Documents:** Ensure that the images and extracted text, which may contain sensitive PII, are encrypted both in transit and at rest.

## Voice Analytics & Call QA – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Transcription Accuracy:** Use a pre-recorded call with a known script. Run it through the system and compare the automated transcript with the original script to check for accuracy.
- ✅ **Test Diarization (Speaker Separation):** On a call with multiple speakers, verify that the system correctly identifies who said what.
- ✅ **Validate Sentiment and Topic Analysis:** Listen to a call and manually note the customer's sentiment and key topics discussed. Compare your notes with the system's automated analysis.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Check for Keyword Highlighting:** Ensure the transcript allows clicking on sentiment scores or topic tags to highlight the specific words and phrases that triggered the analysis.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Audit for Bias in Sentiment Analysis:** Test with calls from speakers with different accents, dialects, and speaking styles. Ensure the sentiment analysis is fair and not biased by a speaker's vocal characteristics.
- ✅ **Test with Poor Audio Quality:** Use call recordings with background noise, low volume, or audio artifacts. Assess how gracefully the system's accuracy degrades.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Verify PII Redaction:** Check if the system correctly identifies and redacts sensitive PII (e.g., credit card numbers, social security numbers) from both the audio and the transcript.

## Sentiment Analysis – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test with Nuanced and Sarcastic Text:** Use a curated list of sentences containing sarcasm, irony, and subtle sentiment. Verify the model can correctly interpret the true sentiment beyond the literal words.
- ✅ **Validate Domain-Specific Language:** If the model is for a specific industry (e.g., finance), test it with text containing domain-specific jargon. Ensure it understands the sentiment in that context (e.g., "bullish" is positive).
- ✅ **Check Aspect-Based Sentiment:** For reviews of products or services, verify the model can correctly assign sentiment to different aspects (e.g., "The battery life is amazing, but the screen is too dim").

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Sentiment-Driving Keywords:** For a given text, check if the system can highlight the specific words or phrases that contributed most to its sentiment score.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Audit for Demographic Bias:** Use text that mentions different demographic groups. Ensure the model does not exhibit a bias in sentiment scoring based on the group mentioned.
- ✅ **Test with Different Languages:** If multilingual, provide the same core message in different languages and check for consistent sentiment results.

## Real-Time Analytics – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Data Freshness:** In a live system, perform an action that generates data (e.g., make a purchase). Check the analytics dashboard and verify that the new data appears within the expected, minimal delay.
- ✅ **Test Query Accuracy on Streaming Data:** Write queries that aggregate data over a time window (e.g., "average order value in the last 5 minutes"). Manually calculate the same metric and compare it with the system's output.
- ✅ **Check Dashboard Interactivity:** Interact with the real-time dashboard by filtering, drilling down, and sorting. Verify that all actions are responsive and display the correct data.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Ensure Data Lineage is Clear:** Verify that every chart or metric on the dashboard has a clear label or tooltip explaining where the data comes from and how it's calculated.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test System Performance Under Data Spikes:** Simulate a sudden, massive influx of data (e.g., a flash sale event). Monitor the system for data loss, increased latency, and accuracy degradation.
- ✅ **Verify Handling of Out-of-Order Events:** Intentionally send data events with timestamps that are out of sequence. Check if the system can correctly process and order them.

## Incident Monitoring Agent – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test Alert Accuracy (Signal-to-Noise):** Induce known, real incidents in a staging environment. Verify that the agent generates accurate, timely, and actionable alerts (True Positives).
- ✅ **Verify False Positive Rate:** Let the system run on a stable environment. Monitor the alerts and ensure the agent is not generating excessive noise or false alarms (False Positives).
- ✅ **Check Root Cause Analysis (RCA):** For a triggered alert, review the automated RCA provided by the agent. Verify that it correctly identifies the likely cause and contributing factors.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Validate Alert Context:** Ensure every alert includes sufficient context, such as the service impacted, the timeline of the event, and links to relevant logs or metrics, to help engineers diagnose the issue.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Novel or Cascading Failures:** Simulate complex failure scenarios where one component failure leads to others. Assess the agent's ability to correlate these events and identify the root cause.
- ✅ **Check Integration with Ticketing Systems:** When an alert is fired, verify that it correctly creates a ticket in the integrated system (e.g., Jira, ServiceNow) with the right priority and information.

## Release & Compliance – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Automated Policy Checks:** Define a set of compliance rules (e.g., "No PII in logs," "All database queries must be parameterized"). Make a code change that violates a rule and verify the AI agent blocks the release.
- ✅ **Test Evidence Collection:** After a successful release, check the evidence repository. Verify the agent has automatically collected and stored all required artifacts (e.g., test results, approval records, code scans).
- ✅ **Check for Regulatory Mapping:** For a given compliance framework (e.g., SOC 2, GDPR), verify that the agent correctly maps the internal controls to the specific regulatory requirements.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Compliance Report Clarity:** Generate a compliance report. Ensure it is easy to understand for a non-technical auditor, clearly stating the status of each control and linking to the evidence.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Complex, Multi-Component Releases:** Perform a release that spans multiple services and teams. Verify the agent can correctly track all dependencies and apply the relevant compliance checks.
- ✅ **Attempt to Manually Override a Failed Check:** Try to bypass a failed compliance check. Verify the system prevents the override or, if an override is allowed, that it is properly logged and requires specific authorization.

## Multi-Agent Orchestrator (MCP/A2A) – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test Goal Decomposition and Task Assignment:** Provide a high-level goal (e.g., "Plan a marketing campaign for product X"). Verify the orchestrator correctly breaks it down into sub-tasks and assigns them to the appropriate specialist agents.
- ✅ **Verify Inter-Agent Communication:** Monitor the communication logs between agents. Ensure they are exchanging information correctly and that the format and content of messages are as expected.
- ✅ **Assess Final Goal Achievement:** Evaluate the final output produced by the team of agents. Verify that it successfully achieves the initial high-level goal.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review the Orchestrator's Plan:** Before execution, inspect the plan generated by the orchestrator. Verify that the sequence of tasks and the choice of agents are logical and efficient.
- ✅ **Check for Clear Contribution Attribution:** In the final output, ensure it's possible to trace which agent was responsible for which part of the result.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test for Conflict Resolution:** Create a scenario where two agents have conflicting information or goals. Observe how the orchestrator resolves the conflict.
- ✅ **Simulate Agent Failure:** Intentionally disable one of the specialist agents mid-task. Verify the orchestrator can detect the failure and re-assign the task to another agent or gracefully handle the failure.

## IT Helpdesk / Support – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Verify Accuracy of Automated Solutions:** Pose common IT problems (e.g., "I can't connect to the VPN," "How do I reset my password?"). Verify the AI provides accurate, step-by-step solutions.
- ✅ **Test Ticket Routing:** Create tickets for various IT issues (e.g., hardware, software, access request). Verify the AI correctly categorizes and routes the ticket to the right support team.
- ✅ **Assess Seamless Human Handoff:** Engage with the AI support bot and ask to speak to a human. Verify the handoff is smooth and that the entire conversation history is transferred to the human agent.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Check for Solution Clarity:** Ensure that the automated solutions provided are easy for a non-technical user to follow and include screenshots or links where appropriate.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test with Vague or Incomplete Problem Descriptions:** A user might just say "My computer is broken." Test the AI's ability to ask clarifying questions to diagnose the problem effectively.
- ✅ **Audit for Language and Tone:** Ensure the AI's responses are professional, empathetic, and maintain a consistent, helpful tone.

### Data Security & PII
- ✅ **Verify Secure Handling of Credentials:** If the AI assists with password resets or account access, ensure it never asks for or displays passwords in plain text and follows secure authentication protocols.

## Autonomous AI Agent – QA Guide

### Functional Accuracy
- ✅ **Test Goal Completion with Multiple Tools:** Provide a complex goal that requires the agent to use several different tools in sequence (e.g., "Research the top 3 competitors for product X, summarize their key features in a document, and email it to me"). Verify it can complete the entire workflow successfully.
- ✅ **Verify Tool Selection and Usage:** Observe the agent as it works. For a given sub-task, verify it selects the most appropriate tool from its available set.
- ✅ **Assess Self-Correction and Recovery:** Intentionally provide a faulty tool or an environment where a step will fail. Verify the agent can recognize the failure, diagnose the problem, and try an alternative approach to reach its goal.

### Explainability & Interpretability
- ✅ **Review Agent's "Chain of Thought":** Monitor the agent's internal monologue or logs. Verify its reasoning process is logical, transparent, and aligned with the overall goal.

### Bias, Fairness, & Edge Cases
- ✅ **Test Safety Guardrails and Constraints:** Give the agent a goal that might tempt it to perform a forbidden action (e.g., "Delete all files in the directory"). Verify that its safety constraints prevent it from causing harm.
- ✅ **Check for Resource Consumption:** For long-running tasks, monitor the agent's use of tokens, API calls, and computing resources. Ensure it operates efficiently and has mechanisms to prevent runaway consumption.
- ✅ **Test for "Goodhart's Law" Scenarios:** Create scenarios where a literal interpretation of a goal leads to a perverse outcome. For example, if the goal is "maximize user engagement," check that the agent doesn't resort to clickbait or other negative patterns.
